## Style Prompt

Dark, high-contrast tech pitch. Precision-engineered layouts inspired by Swiss grid design, with electric blue as the sole accent against a near-black canvas. Typography drives the narrative — large, bold Japanese headlines collide with data-dense English labels. Motion is deliberate and fast: elements snap in via expo easing, pause on content, then dissolve cleanly. No decoration exists without purpose. Every pixel earns its place.

## Colors

| Role       | Hex       | Usage                                   |
| ---------- | --------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Background | `#0d0d12` | Canvas for all scenes                   |
| Foreground | `#f4f4f6` | Primary text                            |
| Accent     | `#00c3ff` | Key moments, numbers, CTAs, dividers    |
| Muted      | `rgba(244,244,246,0.55)` | Body copy, secondary labels  |
| Warm       | `#ff6040` | Conflict / problem highlight            |

## Typography

- Headlines: **Noto Sans JP** 900 (Japanese) / **Inter** 900 (Latin display)
- Body: **Noto Sans JP** 400
- Code: **JetBrains Mono** 400
- Minimum headline: 72px. Body: 36px. Labels: 22px.

## What NOT to Do

- No gradient text or rainbow-spectrum effects
- No decorative borders or drop shadows on text
- No identical card grid layouts
- No cyan-to-purple gradients — accent is strictly `#00c3ff`, full stop
- No animations slower than 0.8s for entrance tweens (this is a pitch, not a documentary)
